# About designtaste-by-oasis

What it is: a small skill that takes you from a rough idea to three clickable wireframes and a clear design-system.md.

Who it is for: solo builders making a marketing site or homepage on their own machine.

How it feels: calm and step by step. You answer a few short questions, look at real options, and pick what you like. Nothing is rushed. You stay in charge the whole time.
