# Taste direction

You pick a feel you like first. Build a thin taste profile from examples before committing to
tokens. Thin means: a few directional words plus per-part verdicts,
not a full design system.

## Sources

1. systemdesignhunt examples (curated feed of shipped designs).
2. User URLs: 2-3 liked links and 1 disliked link with why.
3. If a source is unreachable or no examples exist, fall back to
   curated defaults and note the fallback explicitly.

## Steps

1. Collect the examples (systemdesignhunt picks + user URLs).
2. For each of the fixed parts, record a one-line verdict:
   sections, buttons, modals, backgrounds, color, 3D, animation,
   forms, aesthetic, arrangement.
3. Distill to a thin profile: 3-5 taste words (e.g. "airy, sharp,
   minimal motion") plus 2-3 "not this" guards from the disliked
   example.
4. Stop at direction. Do not emit tokens, palettes, or type scales
   here; those belong to the system writer.

## Output

A short profile: taste words + per-part verdicts + guards. One
page max, scannable, no nested detail.
