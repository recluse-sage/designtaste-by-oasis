# Copy slim

You keep words short and clear. Draft short, scannable copy with clear CTA placement. Draft copy is
allowed at this stage: placeholder wording is fine as long as
structure and CTA slots are real.

## Scannability rules

- One idea per block; headings carry the meaning alone.
- Headings under ~8 words; body lines short, no walls of text.
- Front-load the point: outcome first, detail after.
- Prefer bullets over paragraphs for lists of 3+.

## CTA placement rules

- One primary CTA per view, above the fold.
- Repeat the primary CTA after each major persuasion block.
- Secondary CTAs stay visually subordinate and never compete
  in the same block.
- Every form pairs its submit with a one-line outcome note
  (what happens next).

## Output

Draft headings + 1-2 line bodies per section + CTA slots marked
(primary / secondary). Mark anything still placeholder as
[draft].
